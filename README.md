# Hi there, I'm Max 👋

### Electronic Engineering | Neuromorphic Computing | Optoelectronics | AI Research

I'm an Electronic Engineering student and technology researcher with a strong interest in the intersection of **hardware, artificial intelligence, and computational systems**.

My work focuses on **optoelectronics, neuromorphic sensing, event-based vision, and machine learning**, exploring efficient ways to acquire, process, and interpret information using both conventional and bio-inspired computing architectures.

I enjoy building systems from low-level hardware and algorithms to high-level software applications.
